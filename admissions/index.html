Terms

What you are
signing.

Read this before the tour, not after. If any of it is a problem, better to know now.

How long is the academic year?

September through June. Tuition covers the full year regardless of holidays, start date, weather closures or absence, so the fee you agree in September is the fee for the year.

What is the security placement fee?

A non-refundable, non-transferable fee that holds your child’s place. Given two months’ notice, it is applied to June — the final month of the academic year. It is only ever returned in the form of tuition, never as cash.

What notice do I need to give to leave?

Two months. July and August do not count toward that notice period.

What happens if I am late at pickup?

Centres close at 7:00pm. Without a prior arrangement, late collection is charged at $1.00 per minute past your contracted time. Call ahead and we will make an arrangement instead.
