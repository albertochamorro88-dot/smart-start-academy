Programs · 19 – 35 months

Smart Starters

The year everything becomes a question. We build the routines that make a toddler feel safe enough to keep asking.

Between nineteen and thirty-five months a child works out that they are a separate person with opinions. It is exhilarating, and it is exhausting, and it is entirely the point.

Our Starters rooms hold a firm, gentle structure around that discovery. Predictable arrival, predictable snack, predictable rest — and inside those edges, a lot of mess. Water, sand, paint, blocks, climbing, and the running commentary that turns into sentences.

This is also where the social work begins: waiting, sharing, naming a feeling instead of biting it. It goes better in a room of ten than a room of twenty-four.

What the day includes

  • Preschool hours 7:00am – 2:30pm, or extended to 7:00pm
  • Half days 7:00am – 12:30pm
  • Social Enrichment Program, 2:30pm – 7:00pm
  • Toilet-learning supported at your child’s pace
  • Language, motor and self-help skill building
  • Two, three or five-day weeks
