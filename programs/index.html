6 weeks – 18 months

Smart Infants

The first room. Warm, quiet, and staffed by people who will learn your baby’s rhythm before they learn their name.

  • Nursery hours 7:00am – 2:30pm, or extended to 7:00pm
  • Half-day option, 7:00am – 12:30pm
  • Daily feed, nap and change logs
  • Sensory and language-rich floor play
  • Separate, strictly-cleaned infant rooms
  • Emergency care available for under-18-months

  • Language, motor and self-help skill building

Smart Learners

Preschool and pre-kindergarten. Early literacy, real numeracy, and the habits that make a first day of school unremarkable.

  • Five-day half-day option available
  • Early literacy and phonological awareness
