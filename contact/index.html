Hours

Seven to seven,
every weekday.

Every teaching campus opens at 7:00am and closes at 7:00pm, Monday to Friday. Nico’s Studio, our office, runs 8:00am to 5:00pm.

Hours can shift around public holidays and the last week of the academic year. Each campus posts its own calendar and will tell you at drop-off well before anything changes.

Late collection. After 7:00pm, or past your contracted time without a prior arrangement, we charge $1.00 for every minute. Call ahead if a train has failed you and we will make an arrangement instead — that is what the number is for.
