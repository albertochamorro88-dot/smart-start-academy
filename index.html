Why families choose us

The difference is
the adult-to-child
arithmetic.

New Jersey lets a preschool room run at four children to one adult. We run three, and at Palisade Square and Central Ave we cap the room at ten altogether. That decision costs us more staff than the licence requires.

It buys something specific: an adult with enough attention left over to notice that your child is quieter than usual today, and to do something about it before pickup.

Everything else — the twelve-hour day, the five meals, the enrichment classes, the trips — sits inside one tuition figure agreed in September and not revisited.

What the tuition covers

One number in September.
The same number in June.

01

One tuition. No line items.

Extended hours, five meals a day, enrichment classes, field trips, guest visits and special events are all inside the fee you agree to in September. There is no menu of extras, and no invoice at the end of a month you did not expect.

02

Smaller rooms than the state asks for.

New Jersey sets a 4:1 ratio. We run 3:1, and cap our Palisade Square and Central Ave classrooms at ten children. It costs us more staff. It buys your child an adult who knows what kind of morning they are having.

03

Built around a working week.

Every campus opens at 7am and closes at 7pm. Two, three and five-day schedules, full days and half days, so the week you book is the week you actually work.

04

Checked, screened, and visible.

Every member of staff clears a background check and health screening before a first shift. Contactless sign-in, monitored rooms and camera systems mean you can see the day, not just hear about it at pickup.

A room that is quiet at eleven in the morning is not a calm room. It is an empty one.
